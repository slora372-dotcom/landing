12Cuci Payment Methods: Banks, E-Wallets, Telco and USDT

12Cuci accepts most of the payment methods people already use in Malaysia, so you can top up with whatever is in your phone. The minimum deposit is RM1 and the minimum withdrawal is RM25. Here is every payment option and when to use each one.

Online banking

Pay straight from your bank account through online banking or FPX. Supported banks include Maybank, CIMB, Public Bank, Hong Leong, RHB, Bank Islam, BSN and Alliance Bank. Online banking is the best choice for larger deposits, and it is also how withdrawals are paid out to you.

E-wallets

12Cuci accepts Touch 'n Go eWallet, Boost, GrabPay and ShopeePay. E-wallets are the quickest way to top up a small amount from your phone.

DuitNow QR

Scan the DuitNow QR code on the deposit page with any Malaysian banking or e-wallet app. It works even if your bank is not listed above.

Telco reload

No bank account handy? You can top up with prepaid reload from Celcom, Digi, Maxis and U Mobile.

USDT

12Cuci also accepts USDT for players who prefer to pay with cryptocurrency.

12Cuci deposit and withdrawal limits

  • Minimum deposit: RM1
  • Minimum withdrawal: RM25
  • Every RM10 deposit also earns 1 number in the daily 12Cuci lucky draw.

For the step-by-step process, read the 12Cuci deposit and withdrawal guide.

Payment safety tips

  • Only pay to the account or QR code shown on the deposit page at that moment. Do not reuse old payment details.
  • Pay from an account in your own name so withdrawals can be matched to you.
  • Keep your receipt until the deposit shows in your wallet. If it does not appear, send the receipt to Live Chat.
