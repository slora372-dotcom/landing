i1Malaysia App Download Guide

The i1Malaysia app is available for Android and iOS. It uses the same account and wallet as the website, so you can switch between the two at any time. This guide covers the download and install steps for both phones.

i1Malaysia app download for Android (APK)

  1. On your Android phone, tap Android download.
  2. Log in, or register an account if you do not have one.
  3. Download the APK file. If your browser warns about the file type, choose to keep it.
  4. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.
  5. Open the app and log in with your mobile number and password.

i1Malaysia app download for iOS (iPhone)

  1. On your iPhone, open iOS download in Safari.
  2. Log in, or register an account if you do not have one.
  3. Tap the iOS install option and confirm when asked.
  4. If the app shows an "Untrusted Developer" message when you open it, go to Settings › General › VPN & Device Management, tap the developer name and choose Trust.
  5. Open the app and log in with your mobile number and password.

App download problems

  • The download does not start. Use Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone, and check that you have a stable connection.
  • The app will not install. Free up storage space, delete any older version of the app and try again.
  • You cannot log in. Use the same mobile number and password as on the website.
  • Something else. Contact customer service through Live Chat.

Stay safe when downloading

Only download the i1Malaysia app from the links on this site. Files shared in chat groups or on other websites may be modified copies. The app never needs your banking password or e-wallet PIN.

After installing
