12Cuci Register and Login Guide

Opening a 12Cuci account takes about a minute. You need a Malaysian mobile number and you must be 18 or older. This guide goes through the registration form field by field, then shows how to log in afterwards.

How to register a 12Cuci account

  1. Tap Register to open the registration form.
  2. Mobile No. Enter your mobile number without spaces or dashes, for example 0123456789. This number becomes your login ID.
  3. Password and Retype Password. Choose a password and type it twice. Tap the eye icon to check what you typed.
  4. How do you know our website? Pick SMS, WhatsApp, Recommend or Others.
  5. Birthday. Select your year, month and day of birth.
  6. Referral Code. This is filled in for you. Leave it as it is.
  7. Tap Register to create the account.

How to log in to 12Cuci

  1. Tap Login.
  2. Enter the mobile number and password you registered with.
  3. After logging in, you can deposit, withdraw and open any game from the same account.

Registration tips

  • Use a mobile number you actually own. Customer service uses it to identify your account.
  • Register only one account per person. Duplicate accounts can be closed.
  • Use a password you do not use anywhere else, and do not share it with anyone, including people who say they are agents.
  • If you forget your password, contact customer service through Live Chat.

After you register

Your next step is a first deposit, which starts from RM1. See the 12Cuci deposit guide for the payment methods, or the 12Cuci app download guide if you prefer to play in the app.
