12Cuci Referral: RM10 Free Share and 5% Commission

The 12Cuci referral programme pays you for bringing friends to the platform. You earn a free share reward for inviting people, then a commission on your downline's play every week. This post explains how it works and how to share your link.

What you earn from 12Cuci referrals

  • RM10 free share for sharing 12Cuci with friends.
  • 5% commission on your downline, whether they win or lose.
  • Commission is claimed every Monday.

Your downline is everyone who registers through your referral link. The more active players you bring in, the bigger your weekly commission.

How to share your 12Cuci referral link

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. On the home page, find the Free Share banner.
  3. Tap Share to send your link straight to Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, TikTok or another app, or tap Copy Referral Link and paste it anywhere you like.
  4. When a friend registers with your link, they are added to your downline automatically. Their referral code is filled in for them on the registration form.

Track your downline

Tap Downline Chart to see who has joined through your link and how your commission is building up for the week.

Tips to get more downline

  • Share in groups where people already talk about online games, rather than sending the link to strangers.
  • Tell friends about the things they actually get: deposits from RM1, the daily lucky draw and a wide choice of slot games.
  • Help new players with their first deposit. The deposit guide is a good link to send along.

Only invite adults aged 18 and above, and never pressure anyone to deposit. Referral terms can change, so check the Free Share banner for the current rules.
