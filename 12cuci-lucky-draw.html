12Cuci Lucky Draw: How Lucky Numbers Work

The 12Cuci Lucky Draw Deposit gives you a free chance to win cash every day just for topping up your wallet. There is nothing extra to buy: every qualifying deposit earns lucky numbers automatically. This post explains how the numbers are given out, how winners are picked and what you can win.

How you get 12Cuci lucky numbers

Every RM10 you deposit earns 1 lucky number. A RM10 deposit gives you one number, a RM50 deposit gives you five, and so on. You can see your numbers any time by tapping My Lucky Number on the home page after logging in.

How the 12Cuci lucky draw winners are chosen

Results are announced every day at 9PM and follow the Grand Dragon lottery draw. If one of your lucky numbers matches a winning number, you win that prize.

12Cuci lucky draw prizes

  • 1st prize: RM300
  • 2nd prize: RM200
  • 3rd prize: RM100
  • Special prize: RM20
  • Consolation prize: RM10

Winnings are moved into your 12Cuci wallet automatically, so you do not need to claim them.

Lucky draw terms to know

  • Prize money carries a x3 rollover before it can be withdrawn.
  • The maximum withdrawal from lucky draw winnings is RM888.
  • Only deposits of RM10 and above earn lucky numbers.

Promotion terms can change, so check the lucky draw banner on the home page for the current rules.

How to join the 12Cuci lucky draw

  1. Register a 12Cuci account if you do not have one. The register guide walks you through it.
  2. Make a deposit of RM10 or more. See the 12Cuci deposit guide for the payment methods.
  3. Check your numbers under My Lucky Number and wait for the 9PM results.

Treat the lucky draw as a bonus on top of your normal deposits, not a reason to deposit more than you planned.
