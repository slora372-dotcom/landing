12Cuci Withdraw: Minimum RM25, Rollover and Tips

Winning is only half the job: you also want your 12Cuci withdraw to go through smoothly. This post covers the minimum amount, how rollover affects what you can withdraw, and the habits that keep withdrawals trouble-free.

How to withdraw from 12Cuci

  1. Log in and tap Withdraw.
  2. Enter the amount. The minimum 12Cuci withdraw is RM25.
  3. Check your bank details and confirm.

Withdrawals are paid to your bank account, so make sure the details on your account are correct.

Rollover: why you may not be able to withdraw yet

If you claimed a bonus, you must bet a certain amount, called the rollover, before the bonus money can be withdrawn. For example, 12Cuci lucky draw prizes carry a x3 rollover and a maximum withdrawal of RM888. Your balance panel shows your rollover progress, so you can see how much is left.

If you do not want rollover conditions, you can play with your own deposit without claiming a bonus.

Tips for a smooth 12Cuci withdraw

  • Use a bank account in your own name, the same name as your 12Cuci account.
  • Deposit and withdraw from your own accounts. Payments from other people's accounts can delay checks.
  • Check your rollover first so the withdrawal is not rejected.
  • Keep one account only. Duplicate accounts can be closed.

Withdrawal not received?

Check your bank app first, then contact Live Chat with your withdrawal amount and time. Customer service is open 24 hours a day.
